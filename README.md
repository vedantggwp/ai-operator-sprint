# AI Operator Sprint

**Build your first AI operating system in 30 days. Ten minutes a day. Free — no card, nothing to buy during the sprint, ever.**

Most AI content teaches you to *use a chatbot*. This sprint gets you somewhere different: by Day 30 you have a working **personal AI operating system** — your tools set up properly, your context and memory files built, real agentic workflows running on your actual work, and one public demo you can show an employer, a client, or a room.

The only cost is the tool itself: the whole sprint runs on a Claude Pro plan (~$20/mo). We say that upfront because you deserve plan honesty before Day 0, not a surprise on Day 11.

## How it works

- **[Day 0 — The Commitment](curriculum/days/day-00.md):** you pick your Day 30 demo *first*, then train toward it.
- **Under 10 minutes a day, with a hard stop.** Overdoing today steals tomorrow. Each day ends with "you're done."
- **Every day produces an artifact you keep.** A prompt library, memory files, automations, a shipped build — not notes.
- **Four weeks:** Foundations & Taste → Your AI Operating System → Agentic Work (Claude Code, Cowork, MCP) → Build, Ship, Signal.

→ **[The full 30 days](curriculum/README.md)** · **Site & guided coach:** launching mid-June 2026 (this repo is the source of truth)
