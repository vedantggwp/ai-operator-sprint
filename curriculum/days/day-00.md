## Lesson

Plenty of people "get into AI" the way people join a gym in January. They collect tutorials, bookmark courses, and feel productive. Six weeks later they cannot point to one thing they made. The missing piece is not more to watch. It is a finish line.

So here is the deal for the next 30 days. Ten minutes a day. One small real thing built each day. On Day 30 you show what you made to one person whose opinion you actually care about. Not the internet. A person, with a name.

Naming that person is the most important ten minutes of the month. Think about how people train for a 5K. The ones who finish are usually the ones who booked the race first and then trained toward the date. The date does the pulling. We are going to borrow that trick.

You do not need to know yet what you will build. There is a menu below, and you can change your mind right up to Day 22, when you plan the build properly. The date and the person are the promise. The thing you demo is allowed to change.

Already on Pro, or have Claude through work? You are set.

## Build

1. **Create your Claude account (3 min).** Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and continue with Google or email. During signup it asks whether your chats can help train future models ("Help improve Claude"). Decide that on purpose, not by reflex. If you are not sure, turn it off. You can change it later, and tomorrow we set the account up properly. Claude is for ages 18 and over and may ask for a phone number to verify you. That is normal, not a scam.

2. **Pick your Day 30 demo (3 min).** The menu:
   - a personal site, built with an agent
   - a research brief system for your field
   - an automation that saves you an hour or more a week
   - a small tool your team or community would actually use
   - your whole AI operating system: working workflows, written down
   - bring your own

   Go with your gut. If you are still stuck after two minutes, paste this into Claude. It is your first prompt of the sprint:

   ```
   I'm starting a 30-day programme where I build one small AI artifact a day and demo something real on Day 30. Help me choose my demo.

   The menu:
   1. A personal site built with an AI agent
   2. A research brief system for my field
   3. An automation that saves me 1+ hour a week
   4. A small tool my team or society would actually use
   5. My whole AI workflow, documented as a working system

   Ask me 3 questions, one at a time: what I do day to day, who I'd most want to impress with this, and what I keep putting off. Then recommend one option, with one sentence on why, and suggest who I should demo it to on Day 30.
   ```

3. **Name your person (1 min).** Your manager. A mentor. A cofounder. The friend who thinks AI is all hype. Someone real, whose reaction you would actually feel.

4. **Make your folder (1 min).** Create a folder called `AI-OS` somewhere you will find it again. Documents is fine. Everything you build this month lives here.

5. **Write the commitment (2 min).** Open a text file (TextEdit on Mac, Notepad on Windows), write one sentence, and save it inside `AI-OS` as `COMMITMENT`:

   ```
   By [date 30 days from today], I will demo [my demo] to [person], built in about 10 minutes a day.
   ```

   Read it back once. That sentence is today's build.

## Ship

Today's artefact is your commitment file, saved in your new `AI-OS` folder. If you are willing, post it somewhere your person might see it.

- [ ] `AI-OS` folder exists
- [ ] `COMMITMENT` names a demo, a person, and a date 30 days out
- [ ] Optional: posted publicly or sent to your person

## If it goes wrong

- **Signup asks for a phone number or your age.** Real, not phishing. Claude requires users to be 18 or over and sometimes checks. Finish it and carry on.
- **You cannot choose a demo.** Pick the smallest one a person you respect would actually use. The one-hour-a-week automation fits almost everyone. You can re-scope on Day 22. The date and the person are the parts that stay put.
- **No person comes to mind.** Choose whoever you would most like to surprise at work. A sceptical friend counts. Posting in public counts too, and then the internet becomes your person.
- **You want to keep going today.** Don't. The engine here is 30 small days, not one big one. Stopping on time today is what makes Day 14 feel easy.

## Going deeper (optional)

Put a repeating ten-minute event in your calendar, same time every day for 30 days, titled "Sprint: today's build". One minute of setup, and you decide *when* once instead of 30 times. Skip it freely. The folder and the sentence are what count.
